## What is this whole Anki thing?
- It's not playing cards, almost tho, it's cards for [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) - popular flashcard software, widely used for learning languages including Japanese. You have a front and a back of the card, you look at the front, see if you remember the word/sentence, if yes press `Good` (or `Easy`) otherwise `Again` (or `Hard`) and you'll be shown them day by day in intervals according to your learning. 
- If you're just starting out with learning Japanese I highly recommend [Kaishi 1.5k](https://github.com/donkuri/Kaishi)!
